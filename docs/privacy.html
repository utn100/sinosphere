Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sinosphere Rosetta ("the app") is a Chinese and Korean vocabulary learning app for Vietnamese learners. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Nothing stored on our servers. The app does not have a backend server and does not transmit personal data to us.
  • AI API calls. If you configure an AI provider (Claude, Gemini, or GPT-4o) and use AI-powered features (translation, word details), your text input is sent to that provider's API under their own privacy policy. Your API key is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage and is never transmitted to us.
  • Camera and photo library. The app requests camera and photo library access solely to let you capture or select images for text recognition (OCR). Images are processed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Local data. Reading history, bookmarks, memorized words, and deck progress are stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

When using AI features, text is sent to the AI provider you configured:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • Google (Gemini)
  • OpenAI (GPT-4o)

On-device text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which processes data locally with no data sent to Google servers.

Permissions

  • Internet — required to call AI provider APIs when you use AI features.
  • Camera — optional; used only for OCR image capture.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
